01 / REPAYMENT

Choose your payoff approach

Avalanche directs extra money to the highest interest rate. Snowball targets the smallest balance first. Both keep minimum payments on every debt.

Read the CFPB guide ↗
02 / UTILIZATION

Know how much you use

Utilization is your reported balance divided by your credit limit. Lower balances can support credit health, but no percentage guarantees a particular score.

Learn about credit habits ↗
03 / BORROWING

Look beyond the EMI

A longer term can reduce the monthly payment while increasing total interest. Include upfront fees and leave room for expenses and savings in your plan.
